THE IDEA

Access is
only the beginning.

A connected world gives people access to more information than ever before.

But access alone does not create a future. People need direction, useful resources, practical skills and opportunities to turn knowledge into something meaningful.

NARA is building a digital pathway that makes those possibilities easier to discover.

NARA Principle

Technology should open doors. Education should show the way. Opportunity should make the journey possible.
