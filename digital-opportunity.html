NARA A CHILD IS A FUTURE
THE IDEA

Access to technology is not the same as access to opportunity.

The digital world is changing how young people learn, communicate, create and prepare for work.

But useful resources are often scattered, difficult to discover, expensive, or written for people who already know where to look.

NARA is being built to make that first step easier: helping young people find useful knowledge, digital tools, learning resources and pathways toward future opportunities.

We are starting small, testing what works, measuring what people actually use, and building from evidence rather than promises.

The programme is designed around four practical areas.

01

Learn

Curated educational resources that help young people learn useful digital and future-oriented skills.

02

Discover

Easier access to useful technology, tools, scholarships, learning opportunities and other relevant resources.

03

Build

Practical pathways for young people to experiment, create, develop skills and turn knowledge into action.

04

Connect

A growing network of educators, organisations, technology partners and opportunity providers.

HOW WE BUILD

We do not want to promise impact.
We want to demonstrate it.

NARA will develop through a simple cycle: build, test, measure and improve.

01 — BUILD

Create

Develop useful digital resources and an accessible platform.

02 — TEST

Reach

Put the resources in front of real young people and learning communities.

03 — MEASURE

Learn

Track meaningful usage, engagement and outcomes.

04 — SCALE

Grow

Improve what works and seek partners to extend its reach.

WHAT WE ARE ASKING FOR

Not a handout.
A chance to build.

NARA is looking for organisations that can help us move from an early digital initiative toward a measurable programme for young people.

We are open to appropriate forms of support including funding, mentorship, technology, partnerships, educational expertise and introductions to organisations working toward similar goals.

Any support will be connected to clearly defined programme activities and transparent progress reporting.
