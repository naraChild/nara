Hagel Decozar P. — A Story of a Childhood Lost

A story about hardship, family, education, and the courage to keep going.

Hagel was born into an ordinary family, but life was far from ordinary.

His family lived under constant economic pressure. His parents often had different principles and perspectives, and arguments gradually became part of everyday life.

Hagel grew up surrounded by conflict and physical violence rather than warmth and security. That environment shaped him. His heart became harder as he learned how to survive.

Despite everything he experienced, Hagel carried a strong desire to learn.

His education, however, was never stable. He moved from one school to another, and much of his childhood and teenage years were lost to circumstances beyond his control.

Today, at the age of 25, Hagel is still pursuing his university education while working night shifts to support himself and his family.

His father works, but the family income remains unstable. Since 2017, Hagel has lived with his mother and younger sister, who is also still in school.

Life remains a daily struggle, but Hagel continues.

His story is not simply a story of hardship. It is the story of a young person who continues to hold on to education despite the circumstances surrounding him.

This is only one story among millions. Other children and young people face economic hardship, family conflict, violence, and instability that can affect both their emotional well-being and their ability to learn.

Behind every child struggling at school, there may be an unseen story. Behind every missed opportunity, there may be a child who simply needed someone to believe in them.

NARA exists from the belief that a child's circumstances should not determine the limits of their future.
