Our Story

One Pen, One Child

They Deserve to Be Happy.

Every child has a dream. A dream to learn, to grow, to smile, and to build a better future.

Sometimes, that dream begins with something very simple — a book, a school bag, or even just one pen.

One pen may be small to us, but it can mean so much to a child.

NARA is a small story about hope. A story about giving a child the chance to learn, to dream, and to believe that their future matters.

We believe that every child deserves to learn. Every child deserves a chance. And every child deserves to be happy.
