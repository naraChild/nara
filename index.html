WHY IT MATTERS?

A child should never have to lose the chance to learn simply because life gave them less.

Education is more than a classroom. It is a door to a different future — and sometimes, a small act of care is what keeps that door open.

We can see the need. We can understand the struggle. But understanding alone changes nothing.

There is no other way forward. We must act.
